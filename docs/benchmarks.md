## How to read the tables

- A cell is the median fitness of the runs that finished, then the median wall clock time, then how many finished (for example `3.14 (1.20 s, 3/3)`). Lower fitness is better.
- `timed out` means no run finished within the time limit. A cell stops early after two runs in a row time out.
- `error: ...` means the search crashed. A cell stops early after two runs in a row fail.
- `n/a` means that column did not run this strategy.
- The comparison column is the newest label against the first. For a positive fitness (snake), better means a smaller number. For a negative fitness (trebuchet distance), better means a more negative number.
